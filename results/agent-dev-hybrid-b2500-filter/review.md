**Question:** How does deductive reasoning differ from inductive reasoning in scientific research?

**Expected:** Deductive: ideas are tested in the real world; inductive: observations lead to new ideas  
**Gold evidence (p51):** In deductive reasoning, ideas are tested in the real world; in inductive reasoning, real-world observations lead to new ideas

**Queries (1):** deductive reasoning inductive reasoning scientific research
**Answer:** Deductive reasoning involves testing ideas in the real world, while inductive reasoning involves deriving new ideas from real-world observations.
